## Synthesis
In every verifiable cloud/fog/edge paper the "quantum" object is a string of independent Q-bits sampled independently and nudged by a fitness-signed rotation: a univariate EDA (Platel, Schliebs & Kasabov 2007/2009; "quantum PBIL", da Silva & Schirru 2014). The only residue of the amplitude formalism is a self-damping learning rate (p = sin²θ). Measurement is sampling; decoherence on product states is Baluja's mutation shift / Yang–Yao central-vector mixing (Cobb's hypermutation and Branke's memory are the dynamic-environment classics); tunnelling is Lévy/Cauchy mutation; the amplitude floor is the cGA margin. Genuine mathematical differences exist only in physics-derived algorithms nobody in this corpus uses: simulated quantum annealing (replica-coupled PIMC), quantum walks (ballistic spreading, itself a classical interference effect) and entangled Born machines. Verdict: the cloud-scheduling QIEA corpus is relabelling; a paper should state its algorithm as an EDA/PBIL variant and compare against tuned PBIL/UMDA, Lévy-mutation EAs and SQA/parallel tempering.
