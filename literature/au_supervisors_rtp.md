## 1. Rajkumar Buyya — University of Melbourne (qCLOUDS Lab)
- Position: "Redmond Barry Distinguished Professor, Director, Quantum Cloud Computing and Distributed Systems (qCLOUDS) Laboratory, School of Computing and Information Systems" (2025 Annual Report, https://clouds.cis.unimelb.edu.au/reports/CLOUDS-Lab-AnnualReport2025.pdf); ACM Fellow (class 2025). Lab site https://clouds.cis.unimelb.edu.au/
- Stated directions (Annual Report 2025): research probes "Cloud Workflows and Scheduling … Energy-Efficient Data Centers and Clouds … Cloud Simulation Toolkit (CloudSim) … Fog and Edge Computing … Artificial intelligence (AI) for Next-Gen Cloud Computing … Quantum Computing"; flagship projects "Cloudbus and iQuantum"; 2025: 69 publications, 19 PhD students, "released two new Quantum computing software systems (qFaaS and iQuantum)", CloudSim 7G. New grant: "Methods for Quantum Computing Application Deployment", Centre for Australia-India Relations, DFAT, 2025–2028, $217,354.
- Relevant papers 2024–2026: DRLQ (IEEE CLOUD 2024, https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.02748); QFOR (ACM TQC 2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.04974); TraDE (IEEE TPDS 37(1), 2026); REACH (arXiv Oct 2025, with Toosi, https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.06675); ADASCALE (arXiv Jul 2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15681); System-Aware Resource Allocation for Distributed Quantum Workflows (arXiv May 2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.17944); Stability-Aware Proactive Autoscaling Using Double DQN (arXiv Sep 2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14894); iQuantum (SPE 2024); Quantum Cloud Computing review (https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.11420).
- Quantum: extensive quantum-cloud *systems* work (iQuantum, QFaaS, DRLQ, QFOR, QML-systems preprints). No classical quantum-inspired metaheuristic paper found from the lab.
- PhD: "Join us" page (https://clouds.cis.unimelb.edu.au/join_us.html): students "with over 80 percentage of marks in the last two years" considered; internationals "need to have much better academic record (say over 85%) to win scholarship"; send CV with marks, expertise, projects and "detailed thoughts on how … we can work together" to rbuyya@unimelb.edu.au.
- Overlap: cloud task scheduling/resource management is the lab's core probe list; CloudSim is the lab's own toolkit; TraDE/ADASCALE/REACH show live interest in dynamic workloads and microservice placement (matches the Kubernetes prototype). Caveat: the lab's optimisers are DRL/LLM/autoscaling-based and its quantum work schedules jobs onto QPUs; position QI-MRFO against DRL baselines and stress energy/SLA metrics.

## 2. Adel N. Toosi — University of Melbourne (DisNet Lab)
- Position: Associate Professor, School of Computing and Information Systems (https://findanexpert.unimelb.edu.au/profile/689244-adel-nadjaran-toosi); joined Melbourne June 2024 (previously Monash); directs the "Distributed Systems and Network Applications (DisNet) Laboratory" (https://adelnadjarantoosi.info/).
- Directions: "Cloud Computing, Serverless Computing, Energy Efficiency, Green Computing and Sustainable IT"; iContinuum testbed (July 2024).
- Papers 2024–2026: GreenK8s: Green-aware Scheduling for Sustainable Kubernetes Cluster Management (IEEE CLUSTER 2025, https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11186459/); Resilience Evaluation of Kubernetes in Cloud-Edge Environments via Failure Injection (arXiv 2507.16109; ACSW 2026); REACH (RL microservice rescheduling); Optimizing Renewable Energy Utilization in Cloud Data Centers Through Dynamic Overbooking: An MDP-Based Approach (IEEE TCC 2025, https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10738452/); MoE LLM serving scheduling (2026); Multi-Objective Load Balancing for Heterogeneous Edge-Based Object Detection (CCGrid 2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15400); EV routing/pricing optimisation with Markus Wagner (https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20226).
- Quantum: DistributedEstimator (QNN training via circuit cutting, with Buyya, https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16233). No quantum-inspired classical optimisation.
- PhD: "Always looking for talented, bright, dedicated, and enthusiastic individuals"; apply via FEIT rounds.
- Overlap: strongest Kubernetes overlap (GreenK8s, failure-injection dataset, REACH, iContinuum); energy-aware allocation; uses metaheuristics/bilevel optimisation. Natural co-supervision with Buyya (they co-author REACH, MoE scheduling, DistributedEstimator).

## 3. Frank Neumann — Adelaide University (Optimisation and Logistics)
- Position: Professor, School of Computer Science and IT; leads the Optimisation and Logistics group; ACM SIGEVO Outstanding Contribution Award 2025; eligible Principal Supervisor (https://researchers.adelaide.edu.au/profile/frank.neumann; group https://cs.adelaide.edu.au/~optlog/).
- Directions: "constraint-handling techniques, multi-objectivity, and dynamic environments", "Foundations of Bio-Inspired Computing", methods incl. "genetic algorithms, evolutionary strategies, particle swarm optimization, and local search".
- Papers 2024–2026: Runtime performance of EAs for the chance-constrained makespan scheduling problem (TCS 2025, https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304397525002129); Quality Diversity GP for Learning Scheduling Heuristics (GECCO 2025, https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.02235); Multitask Pareto Optimization for Monotone Submodular Problems with Dynamic Constraints (PPSN 2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10425); 3-objective EAs for the Dynamic Chance Constrained Knapsack (2024, https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.06014); Illuminating the Diversity-Fitness Trade-Off (ECJ 2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.16393); Evolving Hard Max-Cut Instances for QAOA (GECCO 2025, https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.12012).
- Quantum: only QAOA instance-hardness benchmarking. PhD: written supervisory support required before applying; 2027 international SRT EOIs 31 Aug–30 Sep 2026.
- Overlap: deepest fit for the metaheuristic half — EAs under dynamic/stochastic constraints, makespan scheduling theory, diversity; a natural home to analyse purity-controlled move size and decoherence as operators with runtime guarantees. Gap: no cloud-systems work; a systems co-supervisor is needed.

## 4. Albert Y. Zomaya — University of Sydney (CDHPC)
- Position: Peter Nicol Russell Chair Professor of Computer Science; Director, Centre for Distributed and High-Performance Computing; EiC ACM Computing Surveys; profile lists "Research supervision" availability (https://profiles.sydney.edu.au/albert.zomaya).
- Papers 2024–2026: Competitive Fair Scheduling with Predictions (ICLR 2025, https://openreview.net/forum?id=jBYQAtzp5Z); The Cost of Accurate Predictions in Learning-Augmented Scheduling (RTCSA 2025); ElastiCo GPU-cluster orchestration (arXiv 2608.07971); SpecBox (arXiv 2607.23933); HiRL hierarchical RL for edge resource management (arXiv 2605.10443); container scheduling in MEC (IEEE TMC 2025); Power-Aware CPU Cap in Serverless (IEEE Internet Computing 2024). Also: Wu, Li, Wang, Zomaya 2018, multi-objective EDA fog scheduling (IEEE TCC, 10.1109/tcc.2018.2889482).
- Quantum: Quantum social network analysis review (Information Fusion 2025). PhD: USYD RTP international deadlines 11 Sep 2026 / 18 Dec 2026.
- Overlap: dynamic-workload and guarantees angle (online/learning-augmented scheduling); gap: methods are online algorithms and RL; a QI proposal needs a theoretical framing (competitive analysis under arrivals).

## RTP scholarship facts (official)
- education.gov.au (https://www.education.gov.au/research-block-grants/research-training-program): block grant to universities for domestic and overseas HDR students; fee offset + stipend + allowances; doctorate support 3–4 years; "administered by individual HEPs who each have their own application and selection processes". Stipend: 2026 base $34,315 / max $53,608; 2027 base $35,550 / max $55,537. FAQ: "The Guidelines do not require students to have received first class honours … a university may take into consideration previous study, relevant work experience, research publications, referees' reports and other research experience"; universities may spend at most 10% of RTP funding on international students (https://www.education.gov.au/research-block-grants/research-training-program/research-training-program-frequently-asked-questions-students).
- University of Melbourne: living allowance $39,500/yr (2026), up to 3.5 years; full fee offset; $3000 relocation; OSHC; ranked "based on their academic results for prior study and their research potential" (https://scholarships.unimelb.edu.au/awards/graduate-research-scholarships). FEIT PhD page (https://study.unimelb.edu.au/find/courses/graduate/doctor-of-philosophy-engineering-and-it/fees/): 2027 Round 1 (Jan–Jun 2027 start) closes 19 Jul 2026, outcome 18 Sep 2026; Round 2 closes 11 Oct 2026, outcome 20 Dec 2026; Rounds 3–4 (Jul–Dec 2027) TBC; "80% … is not usually competitive"; 85% indicative for applicants with a research masters; publications, experience and institution profile "may marginally increase your score"; successful applicants typically in the top 5% of their class with a completed research project and strong supervisor support.
- Monash: $41,100 p.a. (2027 rate); "awarded based on academic excellence, research outputs and prior research experience"; international Round 1 closes 31 March, Round 3 closes 31 July (2026 date brought forward); "the bar is higher" than admission (https://www.monash.edu/study/fees-scholarships/scholarships/find-a-scholarship/research-training-program-scholarship ; https://www.monash.edu/graduate-research/study/apply). FIT page (unrendered): H1 or equivalent; merit + research potential, outputs, publications.
- Adelaide University: stipend $36,500 p.a. (2026), up to 3.5 years + fee waiver (Conditions of Award PDF); 2027 Signature Research Theme round EOIs 31 Aug–30 Sep 2026; "combination of academic merit, research experience and … alignment with the University's strategic research goals"; "exceptional academic results and evidence of research outputs such as high-quality international publications"; written supervisory support required (https://adelaide.edu.au/research/research-degrees/research-scholarships/ ; https://adelaide.edu.au/study/how-to-apply/research/).
- University of Sydney: $42,754 p.a. (2026), $44,293 (2027); deadlines 11 Sep 2026 (RP1–2 2027) and 18 Dec 2026 (RP3–4 2027); "academic merit and research potential only"; publications must be peer-reviewed and Scopus/ISI/ERA listed, within five years; H1E via MPhil (85) or coursework masters WAM ≥ 80 with a research component (https://www.sydney.edu.au/scholarships/australian-government-research-training-program/rtp-international.html ; selection-process PDF).
- UNSW: RTP/UIPA/TFS ranked centrally on "research excellence, research capacity"; Term 1 2027 closed 17 Apr 2026; Terms 2/3 2027 closed 14 Aug 2026; next: "UNSW 2027 July Release" for Term 1 2028 closes 16 Apr 2027 (https://www.unsw.edu.au/research/hdr/application); stipend $38,438 (2025 rate; 2026 UNVERIFIED).
