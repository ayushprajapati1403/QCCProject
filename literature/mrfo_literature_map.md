# Literature map C — MRFO, its quantum-inspired variants, weaknesses, and cloud-scheduling uses

Compiled 2026-09-22 by live web search (200 searches, ~90 page fetches). IEEE Xplore, ScienceDirect, ResearchGate and IGI Global blocked direct fetches; those papers were verified through Crossref, Semantic Scholar, PMC mirrors or publisher landing pages; anything not readable is marked UNVERIFIED.

## 1. Original MRFO equations (Zhao, Zhang, Wang, 2020)

Paper: W. Zhao, Z. Zhang, L. Wang, "Manta ray foraging optimization: An effective bio-inspired optimizer for engineering applications", Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence 87 (2020) 103300, DOI 10.1016/j.engappai.2019.103300 (Crossref: 1,033 citing works; MATLAB code public at MATLAB File Exchange #73130). Equations reproduced from open-access restatements (Tang et al. 2023/2025; Qu et al. 2024; Zhu et al. 2022; Rizk-Allah et al. 2022) and the MEALPY reference implementation. N manta rays, d = dimension, t = iteration, T = max iterations, r, r1, r2, r3 ~ U(0,1).

Chain foraging: i = 1: x_i(t+1) = x_i(t) + r·(x_best − x_i) + α·(x_best − x_i); i = 2..N: x_i(t+1) = x_i(t) + r·(x_{i−1} − x_i) + α·(x_best − x_i); α = 2·r·√|log(r)|. (Some restatements print α as "2·r·log r" — a rendering loss of the √|·|.)

Cyclone foraging (exploitation): i = 1: x_i(t+1) = x_best + r·(x_best − x_i) + β·(x_best − x_i); i = 2..N: x_i(t+1) = x_best + r·(x_{i−1} − x_i) + β·(x_best − x_i); β = 2·e^{r1·(T−t+1)/T}·sin(2π·r1). (MEALPY implements (T−t)/T — off-by-one restatement.)

Cyclone foraging (exploration), when t/T < rand: x_rand = Lb + r·(Ub − Lb) and the cyclone equations are re-used with x_rand in place of x_best.

Somersault foraging (every individual, after chain/cyclone): x_i(t+1) = x_i(t) + S·(r2·x_best − r3·x_i(t)), S = 2.

Control flow (MEALPY OriginalMRFO.evolve): coin flip chooses cyclone vs chain per individual per iteration; somersault follows; greedy selection after each phase. MEALPY also ships WMQIMRFO (Hu et al. 2022, KBS, wavelet mutation + quadratic interpolation).

## 4. Reported weaknesses of MRFO (with sources)
1. Premature convergence / local-optimum trapping: Tang et al. 2023; Qu et al. 2024; Wang et al. 2025 (Cluster Computing); Zhu et al. 2022; Wang et al. 2023 (Biomimetics).
2. Cyclone/somersault over-exploit the single best individual, collapsing diversity late: Tang et al. 2023, 2025; Turgut et al. 2025; Yahia et al. 2025 survey.
3. Fixed somersault factor S = 2: Tang et al. 2025; Zhang et al. 2024 (IBMRFO); Wang et al. 2025; Yahia et al. 2025.
4. Degradation on high-dimensional problems: Wang et al. 2025; Turgut et al. 2025; Yahia et al. 2025.
5. Continuous-only design; discrete problems need transfer functions or crossover: Yıldızdan 2023 (Bin_MRFOA); Zhang et al. 2024; Bouaita et al. 2026; El-Ashmawi et al. 2023; Yahia et al. 2025.
6. No memory of previous bests: Rizk-Allah et al. 2022.
7. Parameter sensitivity / no self-adaptation: Yahia et al. 2025.
8. Weak as a scheduling competitor: Shao et al. 2025.

## 5. Synthesis — the literature gap
Six years after Zhao et al. (2020), the quantum-inspired branch of MRFO is thin: six quantum-labelled items, two unreadable, one initialization-only, three with a real change of move law (Gupta & Kumar 2024 Bloch-phase encoding; Turgut 2025 QPSO delta-well; QFLN-QIMRO 2026). All are continuous-valued; none is benchmarked on a scheduling problem; the two surveys index different single variants; acronyms collide. MRFO in cloud scheduling is ~10 papers, mostly regional venues, all repairing MRFO's faults ad hoc (SSA, Lévy, crossover, transfer functions, adaptive S), and a 2025 Cluster Computing study shows hybrid MRFO losing to PSO-GA on discrete edge scheduling. No paper applies a quantum-inspired MRFO to cloud task/workflow/VM scheduling; no quantum MRFO exists in a discrete encoding; none releases code.
